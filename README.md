## ⚡ About

WENG is an electronic music producer and DJ based in Canada, operating at the crossroads of hard techno and artificial intelligence.

Classically trained in piano and shaped by the dark, visceral energy of heavy rock and metal, WENG channels raw emotional intensity into high-velocity club sets (150–165 BPM). Her sound combines distorted industrial percussion, relentless rolling kicks, and intricate melodic motifs.

By day, she works in artificial intelligence and machine learning research; by night, she headlines and supports across Canada's underground circuit, including notable appearances at Toronto's historic *Comfort Zone* (System), Montreal's *Pangea* (Sidequest), and *Montreal Music Week*.
